## Structural Notes

**Two-part vs four-part question:** The arc of posts 1-70 actually supports either structure. If you want a tighter book, it could collapse to:
- Part One: The World Gets You Wrong (Diagnosis + Mechanism)
- Part Two: The Path Forward (Development + Alternative)

**The blog-to-book problem:** Many posts were written as standalone pieces for a reader entering anywhere. A book reader follows an arc. Some posts will need transitional tissue — a paragraph or two connecting them to what came before and what follows. That's probably the main editorial work.

**Voice consistency:** The 2024 posts (1-16) have a slightly more clinical voice. The 2025-2026 posts are often more personal. In the book this might actually work as an arc — the author moving from observer to participant to theorist to witness. Or it might need to be smoothed. Your call.

**The mbti-notes material:** The analysis of those exchanges — the systematic harm, the double-bind pattern — could become its own chapter or interlude in Part Two. It's the most concrete illustration of how the mechanism operates in practice, and it's also your origin story as a theorist.
