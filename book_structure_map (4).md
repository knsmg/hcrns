## Structural Notes

**Two-part vs four-part question:** Could collapse to:
- Part One: The World Gets You Wrong (Diagnosis + Mechanism)
- Part Two: The Path Forward (Development + Alternative)

**The blog-to-book problem:** Posts were written as standalone pieces. Book readers follow an arc. Transitional tissue needed between sections — probably the main editorial work.

**Voice arc:** 2024 posts (1-16) are more clinical. 2025-2026 posts are more personal. This works as a developmental arc — author moving from observer to participant to theorist to witness. Could be made explicit in an introduction.

**The mbti-notes material:** Could become its own interlude in Part Two — the most concrete illustration of the mechanism in practice, and the origin story of the theoretical framework.
